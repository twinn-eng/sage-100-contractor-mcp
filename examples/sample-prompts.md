# Sample Prompts

These are written for the demo company, Ridgeline Mechanical & Plumbing. Results depend on the tools enabled for your account.

## Job cost

- Which active jobs are over budget, and by how much?
- Break down costs on our largest open job by cost code.
- Compare budgeted and actual labor hours across all active jobs.

## Inventory

- What materials are below their reorder point?
- Which jobs have the most inventory allocated to them right now?

## Accounting

- Summarize accounts payable aging by vendor.
- Which customers have invoices more than 60 days past due?

## Reporting

- Give me a one-page summary of job profitability for this month.
- Which project managers have the highest margin on completed jobs this year?
